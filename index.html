The intelligence inside your home

Your house is the structure.
Avenlo brings it to life.

A house has rooms, doors, windows and a driveway. Connect Avenlo, and those spaces become part of one intelligent environment. Your cameras become its eyes. Its observations become a memory. Its statistics reveal patterns. Its connected devices let it respond. And you can explore everything in plain English, whether it happened moments ago or weeks ago.

A home that thinks ahead

Not just cameras.
A living environment.

Imagine your driveway lights coming on when your car arrives after dark. A notification when your dog enters the garden. Or a daily summary of movement around your property, with the time of day and weather taken into account. Avenlo is being designed to turn visual observations into useful actions.

01 / NOTICE

See what changes.

Monitor people, pets, cars and activity in defined camera zones, including the driveway, front gate and garden.

02 / DECIDE

Understand the context.

Combine observed activity with schedules, daylight, night-time conditions and available weather or sensor data.

03 / ACT

Make something happen.

Plan automated notifications and authorised Tuya / Smart Life routines for compatible lights, switches and devices.

A different kind of intelligence

Technology should feel less like technology.

We believe the best tools help you feel more present, not more distracted. Avenlo is being designed around useful observations, natural questions and calm experiences.

No endless searching. No complicated dashboards just to find a simple answer. Just a more intuitive connection to the spaces that matter.
