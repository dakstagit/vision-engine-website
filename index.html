A more useful way to see

Ask your home a question.

Instead of searching through hours of footage, ask about the latest available camera observations or historical events.

“Where was Mum last seen?”
“Has the dog been in the garden?”
“Was anyone near the gate?”
“Which birds visited today?”
“What happened this afternoon?”

Examples illustrate planned capabilities. Answers depend on camera coverage, visibility and product configuration.
